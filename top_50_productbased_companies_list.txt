Key Application Strategies for SDE 1 RolesDSA & Problem Solving: 
Most companies require 1–2 online assessments followed by 2–3 technical rounds 
focusing on arrays, two pointers, hashing, trees, graphs, and dynamic programming.   

Machine Coding / Low-Level Design (LLD): Indian unicorns (Flipkart, Swiggy, Razorpay, PhonePe, CRED) frequently include an LLD or machine coding round requiring clean OOP design, 
design patterns, and running code within 90 minutes.  

Referrals Over Direct Portals: Cold applications on portals receive thousands of submissions;
targeting engineering connections on LinkedIn for internal job IDs significantly improves callback rates.
